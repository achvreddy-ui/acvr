B.E. Mechanical Engineering SWM Rules 2026 Compliant ULB Advisory Biomining Expert

Founded and led by Alla China Venkata Reddy, ACVR Consultancy brings over 27 years of hardcore mechanical engineering and solid waste management specialization across India.

Unlike general consultancy firms that rely on theoretical templates, our advisory is forged from direct site execution—evaluating over 205 Urban Local Body Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), engineering complex legacy dumpsite biomining, and successfully executing 14+ major municipal processing facilities across 6 states.

We work as independent expert advisors to municipal corporations, State Nodal Agencies, Smart City SPVs, and private EPC firms executing high-stakes waste infrastructure.

Core Technical Domains

Hardcore engineering services for complex waste mandates.

Engineered precisely to meet current environmental standards, SWM Rules, and state-level municipal requirements.

01 / ENGINEERING

Legacy Waste Biomining & Remediation

Comprehensive dumpsite profiling, trammel sizing, excavation protocols, inert separation, RDF recovery, and bio-remediated land reclamation compliant with strict CPCB guidelines.

02 / SCRUTINY

ULB DPR Evaluation & Technical Scrutiny

Rigorous third-party evaluation of Detailed Project Reports for Urban Local Bodies. Detecting engineering anomalies, sizing bottlenecks, and financial viability gaps before capital outlay.

03 / PROCESSING

Mechanical Composting & RDF Plants

End-to-end plant engineering for windrow/in-vessel composting, Refuse-Derived Fuel (RDF) processing lines, trommels, magnetic separators, and automated air classifiers.
