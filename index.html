Advanced Resource Recovery

Torrefaction — Advanced Thermal Conversion

A thermal treatment process that upgrades suitable biomass and municipal solid waste fractions into a densified, energy-rich fuel product. Not a marketing concept — an engineering opportunity with specific feedstock, economic, and operational parameters.

Torrefaction is a mild pyrolysis process (typically 200–350°C in an oxygen-limited environment) that converts suitable waste fractions into a stable, hydrophobic, grindable fuel product with properties approaching lignite coal. The technical question is not whether torrefaction "works" — it is whether your feedstock, processing configuration, and end-use scenario justify the investment.

ACVR provides the engineering analysis to answer that question: feedstock suitability assessment, moisture and calorific value evaluation, emissions compliance, process economics, and end-use validation with industrial offtake partners.

Feedstock Suitability
MSW fractions, agricultural residue, woody biomass — evaluated for moisture, ash, and volatile content.
Output Properties
Hydrophobic, grindable fuel with elevated calorific value — suitable for co-processing in cement kilns and boilers.
Emissions & Compliance
Process design aligned with applicable air pollution control standards and SWM regulatory requirements.
Economics & End-Use
Capex/Opex modeling, fuel offtake validation, and revenue assessment against conventional RDF pathways.
